# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

Our GTM strategy is hyper-focused on B2B engineering and finance leaders who feel the acute pain of decentralized, unmanaged SaaS sprawl—specifically around AI tooling.

## Target Audience
**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
*   **Role:** VP of Engineering, CTO, or Head of Finance.
*   **Company Size:** 50 - 500 employees (Mid-Market).
*   **Pain Point:** Engineers are expensing various AI tools (ChatGPT Plus, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Pro) on individual corporate cards. The company lacks visibility into redundancy, usage, and enterprise discount opportunities.

## Acquisition Channels

### 1. Engineering-Led Growth (The "Free Audit" Wedge)
Our primary acquisition motion relies on the free, self-serve "AI Spend Audit."
*   **Mechanic:** We offer a fast, frictionless, no-signup-required calculator.
*   **Viral Loop:** The output is a highly polished, "screenshot-worthy" dashboard that highlights a specific dollar amount saved. This creates a compelling internal asset for an engineering manager to share with their CFO to justify standardizing their AI stack.
*   **Distribution:** 
    *   HackerNews "Show HN" focused on the math of API vs. Seat subscriptions.
    *   Twitter/X tech community (targeting "build in public" founders discussing AI costs).
    *   Subreddits (`r/SaaS`, `r/devops`).

### 2. Outbound to Finance / FinOps
While engineers adopt the tools, finance holds the budget.
*   **Tactic:** Highly personalized cold emails to Heads of Finance at funded series A/B startups.
*   **Message:** "Your engineering team is likely paying retail pricing ($20-30/seat) for redundant AI tools. We consolidate this, negotiate enterprise tiers, and typically shave 15-30% off your monthly AI burn. I ran a predictive audit for a company your size and found $X in savings."
*   **Call to Action:** "Run your numbers through our free calculator, or book a 15-minute consultation."

### 3. "Shadow IT" SEO Strategy
Capture high-intent search traffic from finance and IT professionals trying to manage AI subscriptions.
*   **Target Keywords:** "GitHub Copilot vs Cursor cost," "ChatGPT Team vs Plus for enterprise," "How to manage AI subscriptions," "AI SaaS sprawl."
*   **Content:** Deep-dive comparative analyses with transparent financial models (e.g., "When does it make sense to move from ChatGPT Plus to Team? The definitive financial guide.")

## Conversion & Monetization
The free audit serves as top-of-funnel (TOFU). The monetization happens when companies realize the complexity of executing the recommendations.
1.  **The Hook:** The free audit shows $2,400/yr in potential savings.
2.  **The Catch:** The caveats (e.g., "Downgrading loses shared workspaces") make them hesitate to DIY.
3.  **The Close:** "Book a Credex Consultation." We charge a percentage of the savings we negotiate, or a flat advisory fee for restructuring their SaaS stack.
