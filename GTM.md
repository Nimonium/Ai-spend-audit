# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

Our GTM strategy is engineered to bypass the noise in the standard SaaS market by focusing strictly on the acute pain of decentralized, unmanaged SaaS sprawl—specifically around AI tooling.

## The Problem
Engineers are expensing AI tools (ChatGPT Plus, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Pro) on individual corporate cards. Finance lacks visibility into redundancy, and VP Engs lack visibility into actual utilization. The current process to audit this is a messy spreadsheet exercise that takes days.

## Target Audience
**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
*   **Role:** VP of Engineering, CTO, or Head of Finance/FinOps.
*   **Company Size:** 100 - 500 employees (Mid-Market).
*   **Pain Point:** They are paying retail pricing ($20-30/seat) for redundant AI tools and want to consolidate to secure enterprise discounts and data privacy.

## Acquisition Strategy: Engineering-Led Growth (The "Free Audit" Wedge)

We don't do cold outreach selling "SaaS management software." We use a free, self-serve "AI Spend Audit" calculator as a wedge.

*   **The Hook:** A fast, frictionless, no-signup calculator that outputs a visually striking dashboard highlighting a specific dollar amount saved.
*   **The Viral Loop:** The output is designed to be "screenshot-worthy." An engineering manager runs it, sees a potential $12,000/yr savings, and slacks the screenshot to their CFO to justify standardizing on a single AI stack.
*   **Distribution Channels:** 
    *   HackerNews ("Show HN: I built a tool that proves your team is wasting money on AI seats").
    *   Twitter/X tech communities.
    *   Targeted Subreddits (`r/SaaS`, `r/devops`, `r/FinOps`).

## The Funnel & Conversion (The Catch)

The free audit serves as top-of-funnel (TOFU). Monetization happens when the complexity of the tradeoffs sets in.

1.  **The Hook:** The free audit shows $12,000/yr in potential savings by downgrading and consolidating.
2.  **The Catch (Caveats):** Our engine is brutally honest. It highlights that downgrading ChatGPT Team to Plus means losing enterprise data privacy guarantees. Canceling Copilot for Cursor might disrupt teams reliant on Visual Studio.
3.  **The Close:** The user realizes this requires a nuanced, org-wide negotiation. The CTA is: *"Book a Credex Consultation."*

## Realistic Tradeoffs & Imperfections
*   **CAC vs. LTV:** Acquiring leads via organic engineering channels keeps CAC near zero initially, but scaling outbound to FinOps teams will rapidly increase CAC. 
*   **Sales Cycle:** Enterprise negotiation and procurement cycles are notoriously slow (3-6 months). This means the "Credex Advisory" revenue model will be highly illiquid in the first year. The wedge product *must* convert at a high enough rate to sustain operations during these sales cycles.
