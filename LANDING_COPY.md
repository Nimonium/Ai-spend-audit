# Landing Page Copy & Iteration Notes

The current copy on `src/app/page.tsx` was written to evoke a premium, Vercel-like aesthetic. It targets the specific pain of "SaaS Sprawl" without sounding like a generic corporate tool.

## Current Hero Copy
> **Headline:** Stop bleeding money on redundant AI seats.
> **Sub-headline:** Engineering teams are wasting thousands on overlapping ChatGPT, Copilot, and Anthropic subscriptions. Run a free audit to instantly uncover your hidden SaaS savings.
> **CTA:** Start Free Audit

## Iteration History & A/B Test Ideas

*   *Iteration 1 (Failed):* "Manage your AI Subscriptions." - Too boring, sounds like an IT admin tool.
*   *Iteration 2 (Current):* "Stop bleeding money on redundant AI seats." - Stronger, uses active verbs ("bleeding"), highlights the specific asset ("seats").
*   *Future A/B Test:* "Your engineers are expensing AI tools you don't need." - Targets the VP Finance directly by highlighting shadow IT.

## Core Value Props
1.  **Instant Financial Clarity:** (Addresses the spreadsheet pain).
2.  **No OAuth Required:** (Bypasses the InfoSec barrier).
3.  **Actionable Caveats:** (Builds trust by not blindly recommending downgrades).
